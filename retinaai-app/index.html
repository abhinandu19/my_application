Major Project Report A8

Explainable AI for Diabetic Retinopathy Screening in Rural India

Authors: K. Sunil¹, M. Abhinandu², P. Devaraj³, C. Lokesh⁴, K. Karthik⁵ | Department of CSE, KPRIT, Hyderabad

1 Abstract & Introduction

India is home to over 77 million people with diabetes, and nearly 30% of them develop diabetic retinopathy (DR). Manual retinal examination remains slow, expensive, and dependent on scarce specialist availability. This project proposes an offline mobile-based AI system using MobileNetV2 and Grad-CAM to bridge the diagnostic gap in rural India.

2 System Architecture & Pipeline

The pipeline consists of 5 stages: (1) Image Capture via smartphone retinal adapter, (2) Preprocessing (circular crop & black border removal), (3) Classification via MobileNetV2 CNN trained on EyePACS and IDRiD datasets, (4) Explainability via Grad-CAM heatmaps, and (5) Referral Logic layer communicating in local languages (Hindi/Telugu).

3 Technology Stack

AI Model: MobileNetV2 Transfer Learning
On-Device Inference: TensorFlow Lite / WebAssembly
Explainability: Grad-CAM Heatmap Overlay
Datasets: EyePACS & IDRiD Benchmark Labeled Images
